# VORTEX WAR — GAME MODES

- Battle Royale: Solo / Duo / Squad, 20–50 players.
- Team Deathmatch: 10v10.
- Domination: 10v10 objective-based multiplayer.
- Search & Destroy: round-based objective mode.
- Free-for-All: individual combat.
- Ranked: competitive matchmaking with seasonal ranks.
- Custom Match: configurable rules and teams.
- Training Ground: weapons, movement and vehicle practice.
- Vortex Arena: rotating combat experiences.
- Tournament Mode: structured competitive matches and brackets.
